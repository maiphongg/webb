The Hidden Gate · 影の道

Where stillness
reveals the unseen.

Enter Kyoto through its quiet thresholds, where ritual, craft, and memory shape the path.

Enter the gate

Charred cypress, worn stone, one gate left open.

Kage begins where the city stops: a mountain gate of cedar burned black, standing in its own weather. The soot is how a board is taught to survive a hundred rainy seasons.

Climb the worn steps and the worship hall lifts out of the mist, its paper screens lit from inside. Above the eaves a vermilion moon holds its place. Nothing here is in a hurry.
